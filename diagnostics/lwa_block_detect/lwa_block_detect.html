LWA-Based Blocking Detection and Classification

This process-oriented diagnostic derives local wave activity (LWA) from daily 500-hPa model geopotential height, tracks persistent features, and reports the location, duration, peak date and position, horizontal size, strength, and ridge/trough/dipole structure of every detected event. The figures show representative ridge and dipole events in each hemisphere at peak intensity. Color shading is total LWA, gray lines are Z500, the blue contour is the detected boundary, and the star is the LWA maximum.
