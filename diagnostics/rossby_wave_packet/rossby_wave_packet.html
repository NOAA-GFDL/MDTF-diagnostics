Rossby Wave Packet Amplitude and Propagation

The Rossby Wave Packet POD diagnoses the envelope amplitude, wrapped phase, phase speed, and horizontal group velocity of midlatitude wave packets from six-hourly model northward wind at 250 hPa. It accepts the CF calendars and native variable and coordinate names supplied by the MDTF model-data layer, provided that the model field is on a regular global latitude-longitude grid. Full diagnostic documentation

Amplitude panels show time means for every model case in the run. Phase, phase-speed, and group-velocity panels show the configured snapshot so the instantaneous envelope and packet masks are retained. Group-speed color shading is accompanied by zonal and meridional group-velocity vectors.
